# Darlene Flower Shop

Custom flower order page for **Darlene Flower Shop**.

Customers share their flower idea, and Darlene replies with a custom quote based on that day's flower supply.

**Live site:** [dannguyen02.github.io/darleneflower](https://dannguyen02.github.io/darleneflower)

## How it works

1. A customer opens the order page and fills in their details and idea.
2. They tap **Send my order**.
3. The order is emailed to Darlene through [FormSubmit](https://formsubmit.co), with any inspiration photo attached.
4. Darlene reviews the idea and sends a quote by Instagram DM.

## What customers fill in

* Name and Instagram handle
* Phone number (optional)
* Date needed
* Pickup or delivery, with address for delivery (within 20 miles, delivery fee applies)
* Occasion (optional)
* Colors and budget
* Description of their idea
* Inspiration photo (optional, up to 10 MB)

## Updating the page

The whole site is a single file, `index.html`. To make changes, edit or replace that file and commit. GitHub Pages updates the live site in about a minute.
